02 — pico

From the whales
to the roof of Portugal.

23512000150010005000

0 m

  1. 0 m · the channel

    Whales before breakfast

    Sperm whales live off Pico all year. Boats leave from the harbour, and the old whaling museum in Lajes tells the rest.

  2. 0 m · the coast

    Swim in the lava

    Natural pools carved out of black basalt and filled by the Atlantic. Bring a towel, bring a stranger.

  3. 40 m · the vineyards

    Walk the UNESCO vineyards

    Thousands of little stone walls, built from lava since the 15th century to shelter the vines from the sea wind. Taste the Verdelho after.

  4. 400 m · the island road

    Road trips, windows down

    Music on, wind in your hair, a car full of people you met yesterday. Some of our best days start with “who's driving?”

  5. 1200 m · the cloud line

    Walk through the clouds

    Most afternoons Pico wears a hat of clouds. Climb through it and the whole island disappears under a white sea.

  6. 2351 m · the summit

    The roof of Portugal

    Start before dawn, reach Piquinho for sunrise, and watch the shadow of the volcano stretch across the ocean. We'll keep breakfast warm.

by ferry

From Faial (Horta) it's about 30 minutes by Atlânticoline ferry, and there are boats from São Jorge too. Madalena port is a 10 minute walk from our door.

by plane

Pico airport (PIX) has direct flights from Lisbon, about 2.5 hours, and short hops from the other islands. Many travellers fly into Faial and take the ferry.
